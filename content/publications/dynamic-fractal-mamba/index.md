## Overview

Dynamic Fractal Mamba investigates how sequence models can preserve useful
structure across changes in scale. Its central perspective connects the
multiscale organisation of fractal systems with a neural renormalisation-group
flow implemented through a Mamba-style state-space model.

## Research themes

- Scale-invariant representation learning
- State-space sequence modelling
- Fractal and multiscale dynamics
- Renormalisation-inspired neural architectures
